How to use the sim

  • Click a person to use their phone. All shows everything it holds and lets you post or send a colour to everyone; SOS, Countertally (health reports), Taster (water) and Tail Count (family) each show and send one kind of message. Watch it spread. Taster: a 💧 on each level's water recycler turns amber when someone reports it and red (DON'T DRINK) once 3+ households do. A pulsing SOS marks where a call for help came from; its four pips light up as each level receives it (hover for details, click for the sender's phone). Dashed lines join them to their family; Tail Count check-ins are encrypted so only relatives can read them. Each person turns the colour of the newest beacon their phone has received.
  • Click a stairwell to close or reopen it. Closed stairwells carry no messages.
  • Networks: switch the Directorate's Wi-Fi and Rat King (our mesh) on/off to compare them.
  • Countertally (right): sick residents file health reports; the panel counts the ones that reached one phone, next to the Directorate's official number. Open a phone to count from it. Red ✚ = sick (sim truth).
  • Directorate: cut power to a level: its Wi-Fi goes dark and only ~30% of phones there stay on (mesh only), or plant a lying relay.
