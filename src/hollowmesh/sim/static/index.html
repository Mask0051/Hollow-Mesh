How to use the sim

  • Click a person to use their phone: send a colour or a message and watch it spread. Each person turns the colour of the newest beacon their phone has received.
  • Click a stairwell to close or reopen it. Closed stairwells carry no messages.
  • Networks: switch the Directorate's Wi-Fi and the Hollow Mesh on/off to compare them.
  • Directorate: cut power to a level: its Wi-Fi goes dark and only ~30% of phones there stay on (mesh only), or plant a lying relay.
